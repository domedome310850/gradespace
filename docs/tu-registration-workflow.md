## Student journey
1. Identify Thai BAcc/BBA, international BBA or IBMP; choose curriculum revision, major/concentration, single major/minor/double major. Do not apply Thai 2566 rules to another program. Entry year and curriculum revision must be independently stored.
2. Check transcript and remaining curriculum requirements. Separate core, required major, major electives and their subgroups, minor/other electives, GenEd slots, free electives. Record substitutions, transfer/ACC/EXE and repeat approvals separately; actual recorded grade only.
3. Inspect current-term offerings by program, curriculum, campus, code, section AND group, including course availability, prerequisite/corequisite, section restrictions and exact teaching/exam times. A catalog entry is not evidence of an offering. Source dates and tentative/canceled/unknown states matter.
4. Request quota through the faculty owning a quota-restricted subject; then check allocation. Allocated does not mean registered. REG TU lets students pull allocated/plan subjects and add open subjects; current Registrar guide confirms it checks eligibility and schedule conflicts. A pre-term registration confirmation can be made once; later changes occur in add/drop. Confirm, pay in TU Greats App, and recheck registration result. GradeSpace must never claim to submit these actions.
5. Late registration and add/drop follow registrar calendar; approval/fees can apply. Early drop removes enrollment; W is later withdrawal, not F; late W requires approval. Regular max 22, summer max 6, with dean-approved graduation-year exceptions. At least half credits in own curriculum unless approved exception (regulation §§20–21). Repeat passed courses normally prohibited unless curriculum allows; required F/U must be remediated; elective F/U may be replaced. TBS major-required minimum C implies course-specific repeat handling. Do not block F/W retake planning or overwrite attempts.
6. Use course-specific rubric and exam schedule, not default numeric thresholds. A–F grade points are university-defined; S/U, ACC, EXE, I, W and AUD are not ordinary GPA points. Repeated/replacement course credits count once. TBS best grade rule for required-major repeat/replacement is for major GPA, not an assumed GPAX replacement.
7. Final-term graduation application per Registrar calendar; validate credit allocation, GPAX ≥2, program/major GPA ≥2 and required-major ≥C, other university obligations. Honours requires more than GPA (specialized-course grades, time, transfer/exemption, repeats, F/U, discipline); no automatic eligibility guarantee.

## TBS Thai 2566
- 135 minimum credits: GenEd 30 (nine required/alternative 3-credit slots plus one additional 3-credit course), specialized 99, free elective 6. BAcc core 42, other Thai business tracks 45. GenEd level-100 courses cannot count as free electives.
- Single-major quotas stored per track. Major electives: BAcc one of six accounting seminars; FN 15 in finance +3 outside eligible basket; IB/IT 9+9 with no double count; LO two courses from explicit list; OM alternatives; IS explicit list; HR/ER prefix choice subject to faculty rules. MK and RB have no separate major-elective credits; their other/minor requirements still matter. BAcc 21 other/minor: 7 courses, at least 21 credits or approved minor route. BAcc minor nonoverlap at least 15. Other minor route normally at least 15 credits, required minor courses ≥C and minor GPA ≥2; partially completed minor converts to elective only after at least 3 courses/9 credits subject to curriculum. Duplicate course credits cannot count twice. Double major requires both structures and substitutions, at least 150 credits with program ranges.
- BA335 exchange-only; BA343–348 ACC by committee approval, not ordinary freely selectable credit. Confirm eligibility rather than count automatically.
- Remaining: full lists for other/minor elective baskets, minor programs, concentration selection/changes, applicable cohort/regulation and transition details, scholarship/status restrictions, live quota data, approved substitutions, full exam cell audit. The 408-page contact-sheet overview and 99 term table checks do NOT equal detailed validation of every field. No authenticated REG access; actual individual workflows not simulated with real student credentials.
